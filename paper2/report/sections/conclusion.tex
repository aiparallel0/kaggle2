\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented a faithful reproduction of end-to-end DONUT and a
deliberately non-learned modular YOLOv8\,+\,TrOCR\,+\,regex pipeline
on the canonical SROIE Task-3 split.  Under the original Kim et al.\
2022 training recipe, DONUT reaches \VAR{donut_f1} macro-F1 at
$\sim$260\,M parameters; the modular pipeline reaches
\VAR{pipeline_f1} macro-F1 at $\sim$67\,M parameters.  The gap
concentrates on the \textsf{company} and \textsf{address} fields,
where regex enumeration cannot exhaustively cover the distractor
distribution.

The paper's principal methodological contribution is the catalogue of
fourteen silent F1-destroying implementation bugs encountered during
faithful reproduction (Sec.~\ref{sec:bugs}), each with a measured
F1 impact and a source-level guard.  The catalogue functions as a
regression-test register against which independent reproductions of
this study or adjacent ones can confirm that the silent failure modes
are guarded.  We release the full SHA-256-pinned reproducibility
manifest and the per-bug guards under the licence accompanying the
code.

The boundary this paper does not cross is the one between
deterministic and learned field assignment.  Closing the remaining
F1 gap by introducing a learned cross-attention head, a structural
arithmetic verifier, or any other neural component is outside the
scope of this replication study.
