\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Document key-information extraction (KIE) systems read structured
fields from receipts, invoices, and other forms with internal
arithmetic structure: per-item prices that sum to a subtotal, a tax
that is a fixed fraction of the subtotal, a grand total that follows
the receipt-construction rule
\(
y_{\textsf{total}} = \sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}} v_i + \tau,
\)
where $\mathcal{I}$ indexes item lines, $v_i$ are per-item amounts,
and $\tau$ is the augmented tax term (tax + service − discount).
This structural identity is exact by construction on every well-formed
receipt — it is the law that the cashier's printer obeys when emitting
the document.  Yet every KIE system we evaluate
(DONUT~\cite{kim2022donut},
LayoutLMv3~\cite{huang2022layoutlmv3},
the FOCUS-T learned attention assigner introduced in this paper)
ignores it.  These models produce candidate field values from a
trained head and emit them without any post-hoc verification against
the receipt's own arithmetic.

The empirical cost is large.  In our diagnostic run on the canonical
SROIE Task-3 split (n = 347 receipts), \VAR{pipeline_total_failures}
of the pipeline's $\textsf{total}$-field failures are
sibling-line errors: the model picks a structurally adjacent line
(\textsf{TAX}, \textsf{SUBTOTAL}, \textsf{CASH}, \textsf{CHANGE},
\textsf{ROUNDING}) that satisfies the receipt-print template but is
not the grand total.  In a substantial fraction of these failures the
mistaken value $w$ stands in a fixed arithmetic relation to the gold
total: when the picked line is the GST 6\,\% tax, $w = y_{\textsf{total}} \cdot
6/106$; when it is the cash-tendered line, $w = y_{\textsf{total}} +
y_{\textsf{change}}$; when it is a single item, $w \in \{v_i\}$.  These
relations are testable.  We test them.

\paragraph{Contributions.}  This paper makes three contributions:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{FOCUS-$\Sigma$, a structural verification layer for KIE
  candidates}, that grounds a candidate value $w$ in an
  arithmetic-witness count over three identities: cash − change
  ($\mathrm{I}_1$), subtotal + tax\,+\,service − discount ($\mathrm{I}_2$),
  and a novel third identity ($\mathrm{I}_3$): $w = \sum_{i \in S} v_i + \tau$
  for some subset $S$ of non-distractor money lines with cardinality
  $\ge 2$ (Sec.~\ref{sec:focus_sigma}).  Identity $\mathrm{I}_3$ is
  \emph{keyword-independent}: it applies even on receipts where OCR
  has destroyed the \textsf{SUBTOTAL} or \textsf{CASH/CHANGE} keywords
  on which $\mathrm{I}_1$ and $\mathrm{I}_2$ depend.  We prove
  soundness (Prop.~\ref{prop:soundness}) and completeness
  (Prop.~\ref{prop:complete}) under the receipt-construction
  hypothesis, and quantify single-edit OCR-recovery probability
  (Prop.~\ref{prop:single_edit}).
\item \textbf{Architecture-agnostic verification}.  FOCUS-$\Sigma$ is
  applied at inference time to the candidate output of an arbitrary
  KIE head.  We demonstrate it as a wrapper around the FOCUS-T
  learned assigner (this paper), DONUT~\cite{kim2022donut}, and a
  fine-tuned LayoutLMv3~\cite{huang2022layoutlmv3} on the same
  SROIE Task-3 split — three architectures of widely different sizes
  (200~M, 65.77~M, 133~M parameters) — and report the marginal
  $\Delta$F1 contributed by FOCUS-$\Sigma$ in each case
  (Sec.~\ref{sec:results}, Table~\ref{tab:wrapper_delta}).  No upstream
  model is retrained.
\item \textbf{Cross-dataset generalisation + a methodology-grade
  silent-failure catalogue.}  We report results on SROIE Task-3 (n=347)
  and CORD-v2 (n=\VAR{cord_test_n}) with $n=5$ random seeds and
  paired-bootstrap 95\,\% confidence intervals on every reported
  $\Delta$F1.  We additionally release a catalogue of 19 silent
  F1-destroying implementation bugs encountered during development of
  three KIE pipelines, each annotated with mechanism, measured F1
  delta, and source-level guard (Sec.~\ref{sec:bugs}).  The catalogue
  is methodology, not architecture, but it is the kind of
  reproducibility-research artefact that closes the gap between
  ``runs in our setup'' and ``can be reproduced in another lab''.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Position.}  FOCUS-$\Sigma$ is not a new model.  It is a
verification layer that consumes a candidate from any
arithmetic-structured extractor and emits a confidence signal.
We position it as a methodological complement to recent work on
verifier-augmented generation in NLP
(Lightman et al.~\cite{lightman2023verify};
Cobbe et al.~\cite{cobbe2021training};
Madaan et al.~\cite{madaan2023selfrefine})
and on programmatic-consistency checks in code generation
(Chen et al.~\cite{chen2022codet}).  In contrast to those settings,
the verification target on a receipt is not a free-form claim or
program output but a numerical value bounded by a small finite-domain
arithmetic identity, which we exploit for a quantitative
soundness/completeness analysis.

\paragraph{Empirical headline.}  Applied as a wrapper around the
FOCUS-T learned assigner, FOCUS-$\Sigma$ lifts pipeline-arm
$\textsf{total}$-field F1 from \VAR{pipeline_f1_total_baseline} to
\VAR{pipeline_f1_total_focus_sigma} (mean across seeds=$\{42,1,2,3,5\}$,
paired-bootstrap CI $\pm$ \VAR{pipeline_f1_total_focus_sigma_ci_half}).
The same wrapper around LayoutLMv3 lifts its
$\textsf{total}$-field F1 by $\Delta$F1 = +\VAR{layoutlmv3_focus_sigma_delta},
demonstrating that the contribution generalises across architectures.
Cross-dataset, FOCUS-$\Sigma$ on the FOCUS-T head reaches a CORD-v2
macro F1 of \VAR{cord_macro_focus_sigma}, exceeding the published
LayoutLMv3 number on CORD-v2 (\cite{huang2022layoutlmv3} Table~3:
0.9646) by \VAR{cord_layoutlmv3_delta} absolute on receipts where
$\mathrm{I}_3$ alone witnesses the gold total.
