\section{Related Work}

\textbf{Document understanding.}
LayoutLM~\cite{xu2020layoutlm} and its successors~\cite{xu2021layoutlmv2,huang2022layoutlmv3}
combine OCR tokens with 2-D positional embeddings in a BERT-style encoder.
BROS~\cite{hong2022bros} adds relative spatial attention.
These models require an external OCR step; DONUT~\cite{kim2022donut}
eliminates it entirely.

\textbf{Text recognition.}
TrOCR~\cite{li2023trocr} applies a ViT encoder and language-model
decoder to cropped text-line images, achieving state-of-the-art
printed-text accuracy on standard benchmarks.

\textbf{Object detection.}
YOLOv8~\cite{jocher2023yolov8} provides real-time bounding-box
detection; Swin Transformer~\cite{liu2021swin} serves as a stronger
backbone alternative.

\textbf{Sequence-to-sequence pre-training.}
BART~\cite{lewis2020bart} and T5~\cite{raffel2020t5} established the
paradigm; DONUT adapts it to document images without OCR.

\textbf{SROIE.}
Introduced at ICDAR 2019~\cite{huang2019icdar}, SROIE provides 626
receipt images with character-level bounding boxes and four KIE fields.
WildReceipt~\cite{sun2021wildreceipt} extends to in-the-wild scenarios.
