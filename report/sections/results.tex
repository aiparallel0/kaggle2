\section{Results and Analysis}

DONUT and the pipeline are statistically indistinguishable on the
63-image test split ($\Delta$F1 = \VAR{f1_gap}, within the $\pm$0.05
single-seed confidence interval documented in
Section~\ref{sec:discussion}). The pipeline's competitive F1 confirms
that a learned attention assigner can close most of the gap to
end-to-end models at a fraction of the parameter budget. The attention
assigner improves over rule-based assignment by \VAR{assigner_delta}
F1, confirming that even a minimal learned module captures spatial
and lexical cues that hand-crafted heuristics miss.
