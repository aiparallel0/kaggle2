The $\Delta$F1 between DONUT and the modular pipeline lies inside the
paired-bootstrap 95\,\% CI $[\VAR{delta_f1_ci_lo},\,\VAR{delta_f1_ci_hi}]$,
which is consistent with the McNemar reading
($p=\VAR{mcnemar_p}$).  At the canonical test size $n=347$ the
bootstrap CI half-width is approximately $\pm\,0.022$ absolute F1,
so a difference of less than $\sim\!0.05$ is below the
single-corpus statistical sensitivity of this benchmark.  We report
the conservative reading: the modular pipeline at
$\approx\!\VAR{pipeline_params_m}$\,M parameters
\emph{approaches} but does not match the end-to-end DONUT at
$\approx\!\VAR{donut_params_m}$\,M.

\textsf{Date} and \textsf{total} carry strong lexical anchors
(\texttt{DD/MM/YY}, \texttt{TOTAL\,RM\,xx.yy}) that the regex layer
plus the totals-zone HMM segment correctly on the majority of
receipts.  \textsf{Company} and \textsf{address} are the gap source:
both are dominated by spans rather than single tokens, and both
require enumerating distractor classes (boilerplate suffixes such as
\texttt{SDN BHD}, secondary postal-code lines) that the deterministic
pipeline cannot exhaustively cover.  Closing this gap is the subject
of architectural extensions reported in a companion manuscript; the
present paper documents the gap as the engineering ceiling of a
deliberately non-learned pipeline.

\VAR{param_ratio_numeric} of DONUT's parameter budget at training

learned cross-attention assigner is a question that lies outside
the scope of this replication study.
