\begin{abstract}
We replicate two established receipt key-information extraction (KIE)
architectures on the canonical ICDAR-2019 SROIE Task-3 test split
($n=347$): an end-to-end document understanding transformer (DONUT)
fine-tuned under the original recipe of Kim et al.\ 2022, and a
deliberately-modular detect-then-read-then-assign pipeline
(YOLOv8\,+\,TrOCR\,+\,regex with a 3-state header/items/totals zone
prior).  At $\approx\!\VAR{donut_params_m}$\,M parameters the
end-to-end DONUT reaches \VAR{donut_f1} macro-F1; at
$\approx\!\VAR{pipeline_params_m}$\,M parameters the modular pipeline
reaches \VAR{pipeline_f1}.  The contribution is methodological rather
than architectural: we document fourteen silent F1-destroying
implementation bugs encountered during reproduction
(Sec.~\ref{sec:bugs}), each with a measured-or-mechanistically-bounded
F1 delta and a source-level guard, and we publish the full
SHA-256-pinned reproducibility manifest under a NeurIPS-2024 checklist
so an independent lab can reproduce the table from a clean
\texttt{git clone}.  The paper does not introduce learned cross-attention
assignment, structural verification, or new datasets; those extensions
are the subject of a companion paper.
\end{abstract}
