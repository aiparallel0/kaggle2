\section{The SVKIE Framework}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Five structural priors, five modules}

SVKIE encodes each of the five structural priors enumerated in
Sec.~\ref{sec:problem} as a distinct module:

\begin{description}
\item[Arithmetic prior --- FOCUS-$\Sigma$ verifier]
  (\texttt{paper3/models/total\_arithmetic\_paper3.py}) checks each
  candidate \textsf{total} value against three arithmetic
  identities: $\mathrm{I}_1$ \textsf{cash}\,$-$\,\textsf{change};
  $\mathrm{I}_2$ \textsf{subtotal}\,$+$\,$\tau$; $\mathrm{I}_3$
  $\sum_{i \in S} v_i + \tau$ for some subset $S \subseteq
  \mathcal{I}$ with cardinality $\geq 2$.  $\mathrm{I}_3$ is
  keyword-independent: applies on every receipt satisfying the
  itemisation hypothesis (Sec.~\ref{sec:svkie_theory}).
\item[Spatial prior --- zone-prior HMM + GAT]
  (\texttt{paper3/models/zone\_prior\_paper2.py} ---
  the zone HMM is shared substrate ---
  plus \texttt{paper3/models/focus\_gat\_paper3.py}) decodes a
  3-state header / items / totals posterior over OCR lines and
  augments it with a graph-attention head over a kNN graph of bbox
  centres ($k=6$).  Together they encode line-adjacency and
  vertical-zone structure as soft priors over field assignment.
\item[Visual prior --- frozen-CNN head]
  (\texttt{paper3/models/focus\_cnn\_paper3.py}) extracts a
  $512$-dim feature per line bbox crop via an ImageNet-frozen
  ResNet-18 and projects it to the assigner's hidden space through
  a small trainable linear layer ($\sim$50K parameters).
\item[Lexical prior --- cross-attention assigner]
  (\texttt{paper3/models/focus\_attention\_paper3.py}) is a
  learned cross-attention head over per-line text features and
  rule-derived priors; $\sim$400K trainable parameters.  Carries
  both keyword-anchor and learned-context signals.
\item[Epistemic prior --- confidence-gated cascade]
  (\texttt{paper3/models/consensus\_paper3.py}) routes each field
  candidate through the verifier and falls back to a rule-based
  pick when the verifier abstains and the assigner's softmax
  confidence is below threshold.
\end{description}

\subsection{The three-headed neural ensemble}

The lexical, spatial-graph, and visual priors are encoded as three
parallel field-assignment heads ($H_1$, $H_2$, $H_3$) and fused by a
small learned gating MLP
(\texttt{paper3/models/focus\_ensemble\_paper3.py}) that consumes
each head's per-field top-prob plus the receipt's zone-prior
summary $(p_{\textsf{header}}, p_{\textsf{items}}, p_{\textsf{totals}})$
and emits a softmax over the three heads per field.  The gate has
$\sim$3K trainable parameters; the underlying heads keep their own
parameter counts.

The fused softmax is the input to the FOCUS-$\Sigma$ verifier, which
is the unifying layer that couples all five priors: arithmetic
identities check the candidate; spatial / visual / lexical priors
voted for the candidate; epistemic prior decides whether to commit
or fall through.

\subsection{Architecture-agnostic deployment}

SVKIE is applied at inference time as a wrapper around any upstream
KIE head.  We demonstrate the wrapper on three architectures:
DONUT~\cite{kim2022donut} ($\sim$260\,M params),
LayoutLMv3-base~\cite{huang2022layoutlmv3} ($\sim$133\,M params),
and the in-house FOCUS-T head ($\sim$66\,M params).  No upstream
weights are updated under the SVKIE wrapping.

The verifier's per-receipt overhead is bounded above by $35$\,ms in
pure Python (Sec.~\ref{sec:svkie_theory}).
