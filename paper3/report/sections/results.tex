\subsection{Wrapper-$\Delta$ across architectures and datasets}
\label{sec:results_wrapper_delta}

Table~\ref{tab:wrapper_delta} reports the per-architecture
wrapper-$\Delta$ on \textsf{total}-field F1 across SROIE Task-3
($n=347$) and CORD-v2 ($n=\VAR{cord_test_n}$), each at
$n_{\text{trials}}=\VAR{n_trials}$ random seeds with paired-bootstrap
95\,\% CI.  The wrapper is applied at inference time only; no
upstream weights are updated.

The headline observation is architecture-agnostic: under the
itemisation hypothesis~\eqref{eq:item_hypothesis} the verifier lifts
\textsf{total}-field F1 on \emph{every} upstream head, with the
keyword-independent identity $\mathrm{I}_3$ being the dominant
contributor (Table~\ref{tab:ablation_neurips}, row~3).  The marginal
$\Delta$F1 is largest on the smallest upstream head (FOCUS-T,
$\sim\!400$\,K assigner parameters) because the verifier compensates
for the fewer learned distractor classes; LayoutLMv3-base gains the
least in absolute terms but still moves significantly under
McNemar's test, which is the property required for the
\emph{architecture-agnostic} claim of Sec.~\ref{sec:focus_sigma}.

The $+\mathrm{I}_3$ row is the principal lift: it contributes
$\Delta\mathrm{F1}=\VAR{ablation_row3_delta_point}$ over the
keyword-anchored $\mathrm{I}_2$ baseline because $\mathrm{I}_3$ fires
on receipts where $\textsf{SUBTOTAL}$ has been mis-OCR'd or omitted by
the printer (Sec.~\ref{sec:focus_sigma_complete}).  The cardinality
guard $|S|\geq 2_{[\tau=0]}$ prevents the singleton-witness tautology
discussed in Prop.~\ref{prop:soundness}; without the guard
\texttt{models/total\_arithmetic.py} would falsely witness every
money line as its own grand total.  Hamming-2 OCR-drift recovery
(row~5) is gated on the stricter $\textsf{TOTAL\_STRONG}$ keyword
because the Hamming-2 ball is $\sim\!36\,\times$ larger than
Hamming-1 (Prop.~\ref{prop:single_edit}, $C(D,2)\cdot 81 \approx 1{,}700$
candidates for $D=7$).

\subsection{Error decomposition}
\label{sec:results_errors}

The dominant pipeline-arm \textsf{total} failure mode on SROIE
Task-3 — uncovered during the cold reading of
\texttt{docs/RESEARCH\_DECONSTRUCTION.md}~\S4 — is the receipt
selecting the bare-form $\textsf{TAX}$ line instead of the grand
total when the regex $_\textsf{TOTAL\_NEGATIVE}$ matched only
\texttt{tax\,\textbackslash{}d} / \texttt{gst\,\textbackslash{}d} and
silently excluded the bare \texttt{TAX 1.55} / \texttt{GST 0.30}
forms that 26 of 347 receipts use.
