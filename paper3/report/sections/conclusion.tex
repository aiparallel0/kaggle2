\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \textbf{SVKIE} (Structure-Verified Document KIE), a
multi-prior framework for document key-information extraction in
which five orthogonal structural priors (arithmetic, spatial,
visual, lexical, epistemic) are each encoded by a dedicated module
and a unifying verification layer (FOCUS-$\Sigma$) enforces
consistency across priors via three arithmetic identities.  We
proved soundness, completeness under perfect OCR, and a
single-edit OCR-recovery probability bound; we showed that
$\mathrm{I}_3$ — the structural subset-sum identity — is
keyword-independent and applies on the full set of receipts
satisfying the itemisation hypothesis.

Empirically, the framework lifts \textsf{total}-field F1 by
$+\VAR{ablation_row3_delta_point}$ over a single-prior (lexical-only)
baseline and is architecture-agnostic: applied as a wrapper around
DONUT, LayoutLMv3-base, and our in-house FOCUS-T head, every
upstream architecture sees a positive wrapper-$\Delta$
(Tab.~\ref{tab:wrapper_delta}).

The principal contribution is methodological.  The framework
reformulates document KIE as constraint satisfaction over multiple
structural priors, makes each prior concrete and modular, and
provides a verifier whose correctness properties are provable.
Future work extends the framework to invoices, expense reports, and
scientific tables; replaces the rule-based verifier with a learnable
constraint satisfaction module; and explores compression strategies
for deployment in resource-constrained settings.
