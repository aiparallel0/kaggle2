\caption{%
\textbf{FOCUS-$\Sigma$ pipeline.}  An off-the-shelf detect–read–assign
stack (YOLOv8 + TrOCR + a 1.16\,M-parameter attention assigner; total
$\sim$65.8\,M parameters) emits one candidate per field per receipt.
The candidate for the \textsf{total} field is then verified against
three arithmetic identities defined over the receipt's own money
lines: keyword-anchored $\mathrm{I}_1$ (cash$-$change) and
$\mathrm{I}_2$ (subtotal$+\tau$), and the structural identity
$\mathrm{I}_3$ — \emph{this paper's contribution} — which holds
whenever a subset of item lines plus tax-augmentation $\tau$ matches
the candidate within $\varepsilon=0.02$.  Identity $\mathrm{I}_3$ is
keyword-independent and fires on $\geq 87.6\%$ of receipts where
$\mathrm{I}_1, \mathrm{I}_2$ are silent.  The verifier is
architecture-agnostic: any upstream extractor (DONUT, LayoutLMv3,
BROS, $\dots$) that produces a candidate value can be wrapped without
retraining.}
