\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Domain.}  The framework's structural priors are designed
for receipts and invoices with explicit per-item arithmetic.  Domains
without internal arithmetic structure (free-text forms, scanned
articles) cannot directly use the FOCUS-$\Sigma$ verifier; the spatial,
visual, lexical, and epistemic priors transfer but the arithmetic
prior degenerates.  Cross-domain evaluation on FUNSD~\cite{jaume2019funsd}
and Kleister~\cite{stanislawek2021kleister} is left as future work.

\paragraph{Itemisation hypothesis.}  Identity $\mathrm{I}_3$ assumes
the receipt obeys the printed item-sum-plus-tax rule.  Receipts with
bundling discounts ($\sim$2\,\% of CORD), rounding adjustments
($\sim$1.1\,\% of SROIE), and implicit service charges ($\sim$0.3\,\%
of CORD) violate the hypothesis.  On these the verifier abstains
silently and the cascade falls through to the lexical-prior baseline;
no claim of correctness is made on the violation set.

\paragraph{Multi-seed coverage.}  Headline numbers reported across
$n=5$ seeds with paired-bootstrap CIs.  Larger $n$ would tighten
the CI half-width but is bounded by GPU budget.

\paragraph{No new training data.}  The framework's neural components
(CNN visual head, GAT graph head, ensemble gate) are trained only on
the SROIE training fold; no external pre-training or augmentation is
introduced.  The visual head's ResNet-18 backbone is ImageNet-frozen,
which is a fixed pre-training choice not subject to ablation here.

\paragraph{Framework name.}  SVKIE is the name we adopt for the
framework in this paper.  The codebase historically uses the name
\emph{FOCUS} for the broader suite from which SVKIE was distilled;
we keep the FOCUS-$\Sigma$ designation for the verifier component to
avoid breaking citation continuity with the engineering substrate.

\paragraph{Inference latency.}  We report the verification-layer
overhead ($<$40\,ms / receipt) but do not report end-to-end inference
latency distributions for production deployment scenarios.  The
verification layer is bounded above by the bounded subset-sum DP
cost (Sec.~\ref{sec:svkie_theory}).
