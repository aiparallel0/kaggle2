\section{Broader Impact}
\label{sec:broader_impact}

The SVKIE framework targets reliability of structured information
extraction in domains with internal arithmetic constraints: receipts,
invoices, expense reports, payslips, and adjacent financial documents.
A reliable verifier reduces transcription errors that propagate into
accounting, tax preparation, and audit workflows.

The framework is not a generative system: it consumes a candidate
field value and returns a witness count.  It cannot be repurposed to
emit synthetic financial documents or to launder transcription
errors.  The verifier's failure mode is to abstain (silent rejection
of a candidate) rather than to fabricate; this is a desirable
property in deployment settings where over-commitment carries
financial consequences.

The architecture-agnostic property — applied as a wrapper around any
upstream KIE head — has positive impact on reproducibility: existing
trained models do not need to be retrained to gain verification.
This lowers the carbon footprint of upgrading deployed systems.

Carbon footprint of the experiments is reported in
Sec.~\ref{sec:experiments}.  The verification layer itself runs in
pure Python at $<$40\,ms per receipt and consumes negligible energy
relative to the upstream KIE forward pass.
