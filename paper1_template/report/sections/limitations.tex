\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Single-corpus evaluation.}  All numbers are reported on
canonical SROIE Task-3 ($n=347$, single vendor type, single language).
Generalisation to CORD-v2~\cite{park2019cord} (Korean restaurant
receipts) or WildReceipt~\cite{sun2021wildreceipt} (in-the-wild
imaging) is not measured.

\paragraph{Test-set statistical power.}  The 347-image canonical test
set yields a paired-bootstrap 95\,\% CI half-width of $\pm 0.022$
absolute F1.  Differences below this band are below the corpus's
single-seed sensitivity; we report multi-seed means to widen the
empirical envelope.

\paragraph{Inference latency.}  We do not report inference latency
distributions ($p_{50}$, $p_{95}$, $p_{99}$) for either arm; the
training-time efficiency table is reported but cold-start and
steady-state per-image timing is deferred to an instrumentation
follow-up.

\paragraph{Engineering ceiling, not state of the art.}  The modular
arm in this paper is a deliberately non-learned reference.  The
remaining F1 gap to DONUT can be closed by adding learned components
(cross-attention assigners, structural verifiers, ensemble heads),
which lie outside the scope of this replication study.

\paragraph{Bug catalogue completeness.}  The 14-bug catalogue is the
list of silent failure modes \emph{we encountered} during this
reproduction.  It is not a proof of completeness — additional silent
failure modes likely exist in adjacent recipes (different DONUT
backbone variants, different OCR engines).  We publish the catalogue
as a regression-test register, not as a completeness claim.
