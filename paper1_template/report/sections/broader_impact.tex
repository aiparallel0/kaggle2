\section{Broader Impact}
\label{sec:broader_impact}

Receipt key-information extraction has direct downstream uses in
expense reporting, accounting automation, retail analytics, and tax
preparation.  Reliable extraction reduces manual data-entry burden
and lowers transcription errors in financial workflows.

The 14-bug catalogue is dual-use.  It accelerates honest reproduction
by enumerating the silent failure modes that erode F1 without
warning, and it is a useful audit instrument when evaluating
production OCR-and-KIE systems sold by third parties.  We do not
view this catalogue as enabling adversarial misuse: every bug is a
correctness defect, not a vulnerability.

Carbon footprint of the experiments is reported in
Sec.~\ref{sec:experiments} (energy in kWh and CO\textsubscript{2}eq
in kg).  Total compute spent on this paper is on the order of ten
GPU-hours per multi-seed evaluation pass; at typical grid intensity
this is a small fraction of a single transatlantic flight.
