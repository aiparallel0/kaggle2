\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We posed compression crossover for structurally-verified document
KIE as a falsifiable empirical question: can parameter-efficient
compression (int8 / int4 quantization, structured pruning,
half-depth distillation) place the SVKIE upstream backbone at the
same on-device memory footprint as a non-learned modular pipeline
without F1 collapse?  We released a working repository implementing
the compression sweep and the Pareto-frontier evaluation harness on
canonical SROIE Task-3.  Empirical Pareto results are pending the
next vast.ai compression sweep.

The methodological contribution holds independently of the
empirical outcome.  Compression crossover is operationalised as a
binary criterion that explicitly requires both the memory-footprint
constraint and the F1 floor; the rule-based FOCUS-$\Sigma$ verifier
acts as a graceful-degradation regulariser that catches degraded
candidates after compression and routes the receipt through a
rule-based fallback.  Whichever way the empirical Pareto falls when
measured, the framework is correctly stated.

Future work runs the sweep, reports the Pareto frontier, and
characterises the verifier's recovery rate post-compression on
both SROIE Task-3 and CORD-v2.  We anticipate that int8 alone is
sufficient to cross the memory-footprint threshold (DONUT int8 at
260\,MB sits below the pipeline's 268\,MB baseline by construction)
and that combining int8 with structured pruning provides a wider
safety margin against catastrophic compression failure.
