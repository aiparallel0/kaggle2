\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

We test whether FOCUS-$\Sigma$ behaves as a verification layer in the
sense made formal in Sec.~\ref{sec:focus_sigma}: that on receipts
satisfying the itemisation hypothesis~\eqref{eq:item_hypothesis} the
witness count $W(\hat y)$ is informative about whether an upstream
KIE head's candidate $\hat y$ equals the gold total $y_{\textsf{total}}$,
\emph{across upstream architectures with no retraining}, and
\emph{across receipt corpora with disjoint vendor distributions}.

\subsection{Datasets}
\label{sec:experiments_data}

\textbf{SROIE Task-3.}  We evaluate on the canonical ICDAR-2019 SROIE
Task-3 test split~\cite{huang2019icdar} of $n=347$ scanned Malaysian
retail receipts, fetched on first run from \texttt{rrc.cvc.uab.es}
with sha256 verification (\texttt{data/sroie\_canonical.py}; docTR
mirror as fallback).  The training pool is the SROIE 626-image train
fold partitioned $500/63/63$ (train/val/internal-test) by
\texttt{data/sroie.py}; the persisted split lives in
\texttt{results/split.json} and is reloaded across runs to prevent
val/test drift (Bug~7 in Sec.~\ref{sec:bugs}).  Token-level F1 numbers
reported here are macro-averaged over the four-field schema
(\textsf{company}, \textsf{date}, \textsf{address}, \textsf{total}).

\textbf{CORD-v2.}  We additionally evaluate on the CORD-v2 corpus
(Park et al.~\cite{park2019cord}; Korean restaurant receipts,
$n=\VAR{cord_test_n}$ test images, 30-field schema).  CORD is loaded
through \texttt{data/cord.py} from the \texttt{naver-clova-ix/cord-v2}
HuggingFace dataset (CC BY 4.0); per-receipt item prices required by
$\mathrm{I}_3$ are exposed via \texttt{CordReceipt.item\_prices()}.
For the head-to-head comparison we map CORD's
\textsf{menu.price} / \textsf{sub\_total.subtotal\_price} /
\textsf{total.total\_price} to the SROIE four-field schema; the
mapping is reported in Appendix~\ref{app:cord_schema}.

\subsection{Upstream architectures}
\label{sec:experiments_arms}

We instantiate the wrapper-$\Delta$ measurement around three upstream
KIE heads spanning the dominant architectural families in document
AI:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{(\arabic*)},leftmargin=2.0em]
\item \textbf{DONUT}~\cite{kim2022donut}: end-to-end OCR-free
  encoder--decoder, $\approx\!260\,\mathrm{M}$ parameters; fine-tuned
  on the SROIE / CORD train fold under the recipe of
  \texttt{models/donut\_train.py} (epochs=\VAR{epochs_donut},
  cosine schedule, differential encoder/decoder LR, beam-4 decoding,
  best-by-val-F1 checkpointing).
\item \textbf{LayoutLMv3-base}~\cite{huang2022layoutlmv3}: a
  layout-aware encoder, $133\,\mathrm{M}$ parameters; token-classified
  under the BIO scheme over the same word-level input as the FOCUS
  arm (\texttt{models/layoutlmv3\_eval.py}; recipe matches the
  public LayoutLMv3 SROIE recipe at
  \texttt{microsoft/unilm/.../layoutlmv3} — Apache-2.0 — with full
  attribution in the file header).  This is a fine-tune from the
  public \texttt{microsoft/layoutlmv3-base} checkpoint, not a
  zero-shot inference; the published 0.857 SROIE-Task-3 number serves
  as a calibration target.
\item \textbf{FOCUS-T}: our learned cross-attention assigner over
  YOLOv8n + TrOCR-small features (\texttt{models/focus\_attention.py},
  \texttt{models/focus\_pipeline.py}), with $\approx\!400\,\mathrm{K}$
  trainable assigner parameters atop a frozen $65\,\mathrm{M}$-parameter
  feature stack.  Trained for up to \VAR{epochs_assigner} epochs
  (patience=7 on val pos-mass loss, AdamW $1\mathrm{e}{-3}$,
  weight-decay $1\mathrm{e}{-4}$, grad-clip $1.0$).
\end{enumerate}

For each upstream head we measure two configurations:
\textsc{Bare} (the head's native candidate $\hat y$) and
\textsc{+FOCUS-$\Sigma$} (the same candidate after the verification
layer of Sec.~\ref{sec:focus_sigma} is applied; on a candidate that
the verifier rejects with $W(\hat y)=0$ we fall back to the next-best
witnessable candidate by the procedure of
Sec.~\ref{sec:focus_sigma_robust}).  Crucially, no upstream weights
are updated under \textsc{+FOCUS-$\Sigma$}: the verifier consumes
candidates, never gradients.

\subsection{Wrapper-$\Delta$ measurement protocol}
\label{sec:experiments_protocol}

For each (architecture, dataset, seed) triple we record
$\mathrm{F1}^{\mathrm{bare}}$ and $\mathrm{F1}^{+\Sigma}$ on the
canonical test split; the per-architecture wrapper-$\Delta$ is
$\Delta\mathrm{F1}_a \;\triangleq\;
\mathrm{F1}^{+\Sigma}_a - \mathrm{F1}^{\mathrm{bare}}_a$,
reported with a paired-bootstrap 95\,\% CI computed by resampling
per-image correctness vectors $B = \VAR{bootstrap_n_iter}$ times
(\texttt{core/statistics.py::paired\_bootstrap\_ci}).
Per-image significance against the null $\mathrm{F1}^{+\Sigma}=
\mathrm{F1}^{\mathrm{bare}}$ uses McNemar's exact test on per-image
all-fields-correct vectors; we report the two-tailed $p$-value with
Benjamini--Hochberg correction at $q=0.05$ across the
$3\,\text{archs}\times 2\,\text{datasets}=6$ comparisons.  These
artefacts are computed once per run by
\texttt{report.wrapper\_delta.write\_wrapper\_delta\_metrics} and
persisted to \texttt{runs/<run\_id>/metrics/wrapper\_delta\_metrics.json}.

\subsection{Per-component ablation}
\label{sec:experiments_ablation}

To quantify the marginal contribution of each FOCUS-$\Sigma$
ingredient we run the following six-cell ablation grid on the
FOCUS-T arm of SROIE Task-3 (the most fully instrumented arm in our
codebase) using the runner \texttt{scripts/run\_focus\_ablation.sh}.
Each row produces a complete \texttt{runs/<id>/} directory and is
aggregated by \texttt{scripts/aggregate\_seeds.py} into a single
\texttt{ablation.csv} with mean / std / paired-bootstrap CI columns.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.,leftmargin=2.0em]
\item \textbf{Baseline}: FOCUS-T attention assigner alone; no
  identity check, no OCR-drift recovery, no zone gate.
\item \textbf{$+\mathrm{I}_2$}: adds the keyword-anchored
  $\textsf{subtotal}+\tau$ identity (\texttt{models/total\_post.py}).
\item \textbf{$+\mathrm{I}_3$}: adds the structural subset-sum
  identity (\texttt{models/total\_arithmetic.py::solve}).  This is
  the principal contribution; the row's $\Delta$F1 against row~2 is
  the wrapper-$\Delta$ attributable to the keyword-independent
  identity.
\item \textbf{$+$ OCR-drift Hamming-1}: adds the single-edit recovery
  procedure of Prop.~\ref{prop:single_edit}, gated on the
  $\textsf{TOTAL}$ keyword regex.
\item \textbf{$+$ OCR-drift Hamming-2}: extends the recovery radius
  to two edits, gated on $\textsf{TOTAL\_STRONG}$ (a stricter regex
  enumerated in \texttt{models/consensus.py}).  The cardinality
  bound on $\mathrm{I}_3$ subsets prevents over-correction.
\item \textbf{$+$ Zone-gated} ($\textsf{header} / \textsf{items} /
  \textsf{totals}$ HMM prior of \texttt{models/zone\_prior.py}):
  filters candidate money lines by $p_{\textsf{totals}}\geq 0.5$
  before $\mathrm{I}_3$ enumeration.  Reports the zone-prior
  train-fold accuracy
  $\VAR{zone_prior_train_acc}$ as a sanity gate.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Faithfulness, calibration, and efficiency}
\label{sec:experiments_aux}

\textbf{Attention-map faithfulness.}  The interpretability claim made
about the FOCUS-T cross-attention map (Sec.~\ref{sec:method_pipeline})
is quantified through deletion-AUC and insertion-AUC computed by
\texttt{models/attention\_faithfulness.py}, following the RISE
protocol~\cite{petsiuk2018rise} and the rationale-extraction
benchmark of DeYoung et al.~\cite{deyoung2020eraser}.  We report
\VAR{faithfulness_deletion_auc} (deletion; lower is better) and
\VAR{faithfulness_insertion_auc} (insertion; higher is better) on
the SROIE test split; the random-token baseline lies at
\VAR{faithfulness_random_baseline}.

\textbf{Calibration.}  We report Expected Calibration Error
(ECE)~\cite{naeini2015ece}, Maximum Calibration Error
(MCE)~\cite{guo2017calibration}, and Brier
score~\cite{brier1950score} on the per-field confidence emitted by
each arm.  The per-field ECE on \textsf{total} pre- and
post-FOCUS-$\Sigma$ is reported in Table~\ref{tab:calibration}; the
post-$\Sigma$ confidence is the witness count $W(\hat y)$ rescaled
to $[0,1]$, which is well-calibrated by construction
(Prop.~\ref{prop:complete}).

\textbf{Inference latency and verification overhead.}  We measure
end-to-end inference latency at $p_{50}$, $p_{95}$, $p_{99}$ on a
single RTX\,4090 in two regimes: cold-start (first 10 images) and
steady-state (images 50--250 of the canonical split).  The
verification-layer overhead is the per-receipt cost of materialising
the subset-sum target set $T$ and probing the candidate; on receipts
with $|\mathcal{I}|\le 30$ items and per-item value bounded at
$5{,}000$\,RM the bounded knapsack DP runs in
$\le 35\,$ms in pure Python (Sec.~\ref{sec:focus_sigma_complexity}),
strictly below the $\sim\!100\,$ms TrOCR forward pass that precedes it.

\subsection{Statistical protocol and pre-registration}
\label{sec:experiments_stats}

Headline numbers are reported as the cross-seed mean over
$n_{\text{trials}}=\VAR{n_trials}$ random seeds
(\VAR{seeds_used}), with paired-bootstrap 95\,\% CI on every
$\Delta$F1 cell ($B=\VAR{bootstrap_n_iter}$ resamples) and McNemar's
exact test on per-image correctness with Benjamini--Hochberg
correction across the wrapper-$\Delta$ comparisons.  Pre-registered
hypotheses, decision rules, and the file paths under which they are
adjudicated live in \texttt{docs/archive/PRE\_REGISTRATION.md}; the
hypotheses tested in Tables~\ref{tab:wrapper_delta},
\ref{tab:ablation_neurips}, \ref{tab:error_decomp},
\ref{tab:faithfulness}, and~\ref{tab:calibration} were registered
before any cross-architecture, cross-dataset, multi-seed run was
executed.  The promotion criteria of \texttt{docs/HONESTY.md}~\S5
gate which of these tables resolve to measured values vs which
remain pre-registered placeholders in the current build; the
\texttt{unresolved\_vars.json} sidecar enumerates every
$\backslash$VAR$\{\}$ key whose value is not yet measured.

\textbf{Hardware.}  RTX\,4090 (24\,GB VRAM) on vast.ai, CUDA 12.x.
Per-stage GPU utilisation, peak VRAM, wall-clock time, energy (kWh)
and CO$_2$eq (kg) are sampled at 5\,s intervals via
\texttt{nvidia-smi}\,+\,\texttt{psutil} and persisted to
\texttt{runs/<id>/telemetry\_*.jsonl} following Strubell et
al.~\cite{strubell2019energy}; per-run cost in USD is recorded in
\texttt{runs/<id>/cost\_*.json}.  Carbon intensity is set to
$0.4$\,kg\,CO$_2$/kWh.

\textbf{Reproducibility.}  Deterministic RNG seeding across Python,
NumPy, and PyTorch with CUDNN determinism enabled
(\texttt{core/seed.py::seed\_everything}).  DataLoader workers use a
seeded \texttt{worker\_init\_fn} and a per-worker
\texttt{torch.Generator}.  Every run writes a SHA-256 manifest
(\texttt{runs/<id>/MANIFEST.json}) over every artefact the paper
cites, the git SHA of the producing tree
(\texttt{runs/<id>/env/git\_sha.txt}), and a
\texttt{config\_snapshot.json} of the exact \texttt{ExpConfig} that
was constructed.  The full NeurIPS-2024 reproducibility checklist is
filled in Appendix~\ref{sec:repro_checklist}.
