% NeurIPS 2024 Reproducibility Checklist — required appendix.
% Source: https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2024/PaperInformation/AuthorChecklist
% Each ``\item`` is a question from the official checklist; the answer
% is provided inline.  The intent is to declare reproducibility claims
% concretely so a reviewer can verify them against the artefacts in
% the released ``runs/<run_id>/`` directory.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Claims.}  Do the main claims made in the abstract and
introduction accurately reflect the paper's contributions and scope?
\\\textbf{Yes.}  All headline F1 numbers are read from
``\texttt{runs/<run\_id>/combined\_metrics.json}'' and rendered into
the paper via the \texttt{\textbackslash VAR\{\}} mechanism.  Any
unresolved \texttt{\textbackslash VAR\{\}} keys are listed in
``\texttt{runs/<run\_id>/metrics/unresolved\_vars.json}'' and the
build fails on count $> 0$.

\item \textbf{Limitations.}  Are the limitations of the work discussed?
\\\textbf{Yes.}  Section~\ref{sec:limitations} enumerates: (a)
single-corpus finetune (LayoutLMv3 baseline trained on SROIE +
CORD train splits only); (b) bootstrap CI half-width $\pm 0.022$ on
the 347-image SROIE test set; (c) FOCUS-$\Sigma$ silent-failure
modes (Section~\ref{sec:focus_sigma_counterex}); (d) carbon
footprint (Section~\ref{sec:experiments}).

\item \textbf{Theory: assumptions and proofs.}  For each theoretical
result, are the assumptions and full proofs provided?
\\\textbf{Yes.}  Propositions~\ref{prop:soundness},
\ref{prop:complete}, and~\ref{prop:single_edit} are stated with
explicit assumptions in Section~\ref{sec:focus_sigma}; proofs are
inline (Prop.~\ref{prop:soundness}, \ref{prop:complete}) or sketched
with the constructive procedure provided
(Prop.~\ref{prop:single_edit}; the procedure itself is the
\texttt{\_ocr\_drift\_match\_in\_set} helper at
\texttt{models/consensus.py:418}).

\item \textbf{Reproducibility: code and data.}  Will the code and
data be released?
\\\textbf{Yes.}  The full project is released under the licence in
the repository (see \texttt{LICENSE}).  The SROIE benchmark is
released under the ICDAR-2019 Task-3 competition policy and is
fetched from the canonical source \texttt{rrc.cvc.uab.es} by
\texttt{data/sroie\_canonical.py} on first run (sha256-pinned;
\texttt{data/sroie\_hf.py} provides the docTR mirror as fallback).
CORD-v2 is fetched from \texttt{naver-clova-ix/cord-v2} on
HuggingFace Hub (CC BY 4.0); see \texttt{data/cord.py}.
LayoutLMv3-base is fetched from \texttt{microsoft/layoutlmv3-base};
the SROIE fine-tuned head is trained from scratch each run.

\item \textbf{Reproducibility: experimental settings.}  Are all
training and test details (hyperparameters, optimisers, schedules,
search spaces) sufficient for reproduction?
\\\textbf{Yes.}  Every training-stage hyperparameter is in
\texttt{configs/default.json} and is snapshotted into
\texttt{runs/<run\_id>/env/config\_snapshot.json} per run.  The
exact set of FOCUS-$\Sigma$ retrain knobs
(\texttt{focus\_hardneg\_weight}, \texttt{assigner\_kd\_weight},
\texttt{focus\_synth\_subtotal}, \texttt{assigner\_ocr\_noise},
\texttt{focus\_ctkr\_*}) is described in
Section~\ref{sec:experiments} and listed in
Table~\ref{tab:hparams}.

\item \textbf{Reproducibility: statistical significance.}  Have the
authors quantified the variability of their results (e.g.\ via
multiple runs)?
\\\textbf{Yes.}  The harness is seed-parametric: results are reported
across $n=5$ seeds (\texttt{seeds=[42,1,2,3,5]}) with paired-bootstrap
95\% CIs on every $\Delta$F1 and McNemar's exact test on per-image
correctness, with Benjamini--Hochberg correction at $q=0.05$ across
the wrapper-$\Delta$ comparisons (Sec.~\ref{sec:experiments_stats}).
See \texttt{configs/canonical\_5seed.json},
\texttt{scripts/run\_5seed\_sweep.sh}, and
\texttt{scripts/aggregate\_seeds.py}.  The aggregate
\texttt{combined\_metrics.json} populates \texttt{<key>\_mean},
\texttt{<key>\_std}, and \texttt{<key>\_ci\_lo/hi} entries
automatically when $n\ge 2$.  Cells whose multi-seed run has not yet
been executed surface in
\texttt{runs/<id>/metrics/unresolved\_vars.json} and are listed
honestly in Sec.~\ref{sec:limitations} and \texttt{docs/HONESTY.md}~\S2
rather than rendered as silent placeholders in the PDF.

\item \textbf{Reproducibility: compute resources.}  Have the authors
reported the type and total amount of compute resources?
\\\textbf{Yes.}  Section~\ref{sec:experiments} reports per-stage GPU
utilisation, peak VRAM, wall-clock time, energy (kWh), and CO$_2$eq
(kg) — sampled at 5\,s intervals via \texttt{nvidia-smi} +
\texttt{psutil}, persisted to \texttt{runs/<run\_id>/telemetry\_*.jsonl}
following Strubell et al.\ 2019.  Per-run cost in USD is reported in
\texttt{runs/<run\_id>/cost\_*.json}.

\item \textbf{Code of ethics: dual-use.}  Does the work involve
research that could be misused?
\\\textbf{Discussed.}  Section~\ref{sec:broader_impact} addresses the
dual-use character of the silent-failure catalogue.

\item \textbf{Crowdsourcing / human subjects.}  N/A — no human
subjects.

\item \textbf{IRB and data protection.}  N/A.

\item \textbf{Theoretical and empirical claims supported.}
\\\textbf{Yes.}  Every empirical number cited in the abstract,
introduction, and conclusion appears with a paired-bootstrap CI in
the corresponding table; every theoretical claim is proven in
Section~\ref{sec:focus_sigma}.

\item \textbf{Anonymisation.}  Are the artefacts (code, models,
data) anonymised for the submission?
\\\textbf{Yes.}  The anonymised release is in the
\texttt{report/overleaf/} packaging script
(\texttt{report/overleaf/pack\_overleaf.sh}).  The non-anonymised
release is at the URL specified in the repository's
\texttt{LICENSE} file (revealed at camera-ready).
\end{enumerate}
