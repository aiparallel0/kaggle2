\section{Implementation-Failure Atlas}
\label{sec:bugs}

The SVKIE framework is built on top of a multi-stage pipeline whose
implementation surface admits a number of silent failure modes.  We
catalogue sixteen pitfalls encountered during framework development;
each is annotated with mechanism and a source-level guard.  All
guards are regression-tested in \texttt{paper3/tests/}.  Bugs 1--14
are upstream pipeline failures (DONUT / YOLO / TrOCR training and
inference); Bugs 15, 16 are framework-specific failures introduced
by the multi-prior ensemble and the FOCUS-$\Sigma$ verifier
respectively.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Bug 1 --- \texttt{lm\_head} weight-tying drift.}  Adding
      special tokens before resizing causes \texttt{safetensors}
      deduplication; lm\_head points at stale rows.  Guard:
      \texttt{tie\_word\_embeddings=False} + tensor clone.

\item \textbf{Bug 2 --- decoder start-token list-vs-string form.}
      Single-string input to \texttt{convert\_tokens\_to\_ids}
      collapses generation.  Guard: list-form conversion + assert.

\item \textbf{Bug 3 --- decoder kwargs leak.}  Inference kwargs
      override trained settings.  Guard: explicit whitelist.

\item \textbf{Bug 4 --- fp16 overflow without grad-clip.}  bf16
      fallback to fp16 NaN-cascades without
      \texttt{max\_grad\_norm}.  Guard: precision auto-detect +
      grad-clip enforcement.

\item \textbf{Bug 5 --- YOLO image-size train/inference mismatch.}
      Default 640 inference vs 1024 training degrades mAP.  Guard:
      parity assertion in \texttt{pipeline\_meta.json}.

\item \textbf{Bug 6 --- TrOCR underfit on $<$5 epochs.}  Guard:
      config-loader floor at 5.

\item \textbf{Bug 7 --- split non-persistence.}  Guard:
      \texttt{results/split.json} reload.

\item \textbf{Bug 8 --- duplicated decoder-prefix tag.}  Guard: drop
      leading tag from labels.

\item \textbf{Bug 9 --- stale \texttt{generation\_config} after
      fine-tune.}  Guard: explicit persistence hook.

\item \textbf{Bug 10 --- weight-tie drift assertion.}  Guard:
      assert distinct \texttt{data\_ptr}.

\item \textbf{Bug 11 --- transformers $\geq$4.48 kwargs leak.}
      Guard: \texttt{accepts\_loss\_kwargs=False}.

\item \textbf{Bug 12 --- inference-time \texttt{do\_sample=True}.}
      Guard: explicit greedy.

\item \textbf{Bug 13 --- \texttt{warmup\_steps} silently overrides
      \texttt{warmup\_ratio}.}  Guard: explicit zeroing.

\item \textbf{Bug 14 --- regex word-boundary on Bahasa keywords.}
      Guard: extended regex bank.

\item \textbf{Bug 15 --- assigner head trained but not on dispatch
      path.}  A trained FOCUS-T head shipped in
      \texttt{assigner.pt} can fail to be invoked at inference for an
      entire release cycle if the dispatch test does not exist.
      Guard: integration test that every learned head registered in
      \texttt{\_architecture\_config} appears on the dispatch path of
      \texttt{\_assign\_learned\_with\_attn}.

\item \textbf{Bug 16 --- contrastive top-K repulsion misfires under
      empty assigner outputs.}  When the assigner returns no
      candidates the CTKR loss contains a division by zero.  Guard:
      empty-batch skip + log.
\end{itemize}

The full per-bug $\Delta$F1 atlas is in
\texttt{paper3/results/bug\_atlas.json}; each row carries a measured
or mechanistically-bounded F1 delta with paired-bootstrap CI.
